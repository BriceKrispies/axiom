Claude of Duty

Click to lock the pointer, Esc to release. WASD move, Shift sprint (double-tap for tactical), C crouch, Z prone, Space jump, Q/E lean.

Bare ground: ground.js's terrain, road, kerbs, pavements and alleys, lit by the ported atmosphere model at 09:30. The buildings, props and dressing are not ported yet, and the rifle on the ground is placed as an object — there is no viewmodel rig.
