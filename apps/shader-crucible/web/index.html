THE SHADER CRUCIBLE

Ten labelled stations demonstrating Axiom's procedural appearance system. Every pattern, mask, blend, displacement and density below is an authored FieldGraph over the 27-operator algebra — there is no WGSL in this app and no Rust in it that computes a colour. The stations stand in two rows of six, front row left to right first — drag to orbit them, scroll or pinch to zoom, shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Open the console for the full report: the barrier's program count, supported_by for both backend profiles, and each station's explain / digest / diff.

1 Layered material Brushed metal + paint + scratches + dirt, each masked. Mask-driven layering flattening to Mix composition — the case the whole Surface design exists to serve. Flattened: 58 nodes on base colour, 50 on roughness, 38–39 on each of the other five. LIMITATION 4 — metallic changes no pixel. This body binds Metallic to 1.0 on the metal and 0.0 on the paint and dirt. The channel is carried, digested and reported, and no lighting model reads it. It is labelled rather than omitted.
2 Live procedural surface A field-authored base colour and roughness, evaluated per pixel: graph → Surface → surface_program → WGSL. This is the half of the system no shipping app had proven.
3 Baked texture The same graph as station 2, baked once through TextureOp::Field to a 128² tile. One graph, two realisations; they agree to within one byte level at every texel. The bake writes LINEAR bytes and the albedo upload path binds them as Rgba8UnormSrgb, so the sampler decodes them through the sRGB curve and the baked tile reads darker than the live surface beside it. The graphs agree; the two upload conventions do not.
4 Parameter retune Three knobs on parameter slots. Nine different tunings handed to the preparation barrier compile one program, because all nine have the same digest — a retune is a uniform write, never a recompile.
5 Time-varying displacement Wind (the cube) and ripple (the ball beside it) as authored Vec3 displacement graphs over Point and Time. The clock is the engine's frame time, never a wall clock. LIMITATION 1 — a displaced vertex casts an UNDISPLACED shadow. The shadow depth pre-pass is a separate WGSL module and runs no displacement program. Watch the wind cube lean and its shadow stay standing.

LIMITATION 2 — skinned geometry always gets the default program. SkinnedGpuDraw carries no surface_program lane, and the skinned vertex stage is at the 16-attribute ceiling so it runs no displacement program either. The crucible therefore shows no skinned body at all — there is no such thing as a surfaced one. The console report prints the backend's own answer: skinned degradations: [ProceduralSurface].
6 Three lighting models The identical appearance graph under Unlit, Lambert and LambertSpecular — the closed discriminant, and zero extra pipelines. Three digests, two pipeline markers, one lit program.
7 Implicit surface A metaball body whose shape is a FieldGraph: sampled onto a 40³ lattice as a ScalarField and marched by implicit_surface_mesh into 2,816 triangles. Its colour reads the same density its silhouette came from. The blobs are exp(-k·d²), not the textbook k/d²: the algebra has no Div, deliberately, and Pow(x,-1) is documented to yield 0 at and below zero — which at a blob's centre is exactly the wrong answer. The reciprocal turned out not to be needed.
8 Transcendental patterns Marble veining (22 nodes) and wood grain (26 nodes), both authored over Sin and Pow. A visual effect is a graph, never a Rust function.
9 Both backends Per-pixel on the GPU arm; per-triangle-centroid on Canvas2D. supported_by returns true for every station on both real profiles — the software arm substitutes, it does not drop. LIMITATION 3 — Canvas2D shades ONE sample per triangle. A mask finer than a triangle is not sampled at all, so station 1's scratch lines can vanish there entirely. The meshes are deliberately not tessellated to hide it: subdividing until the software arm resolved them would be measuring a mesh instead of a backend.
10 Introspection The graph is machine-readable data. The console prints station 4's digest, its explain text one line per node, and two diffs: a retune (empty — nothing structural changed) and marble against wood (+3 −0 ~20).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

  1. A displaced vertex casts an undisplaced shadow. The shadow pass runs no displacement program. Visible at station 5. Fixing it is an engine change.
  2. Skinned geometry always gets the default program. SkinnedGpuDraw has no surface_program lane. No skinned body is shown, because a surfaced one does not exist; the backend's report says so instead.
  3. Canvas2D shades per triangle. Station 1's scratch mask can disappear entirely on the software arm. Not tessellated around.
  4. metallic changes no pixel. Carried and inert by design. Labelled, not omitted.
